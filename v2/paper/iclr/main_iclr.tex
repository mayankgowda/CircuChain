\title{The Clockwise Prior: Silent, Directed Convention Reversion in Language Models}

\author{Anonymous authors\\Paper under double-blind review}


\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
When a language model is instructed to use a sign convention that conflicts with the
convention dominant in its training text, what does it do? Across three exactly-verified
physics and mathematics domains (mesh-current circuit analysis, oriented line integrals,
and three-dimensional vector mechanics), eight instructable conventions, and fifteen
model configurations spanning a $\sim$790$\times$ competence range, we find that
sign-convention failures are \emph{silent and directed}: among \totalDiagVars{}
magnitude-correct diagnostic variables, \coherencePct\% of all sign errors reproduce the
trained convention's value exactly, and on the seven pure sign-flip conventions the rate
is 100\%. Whether reversion occurs is governed by three measurable factors. (1)
\emph{Reversal pedagogy}: conventions whose reversal is absent from textbooks (clockwise
mesh currents) defeat every frontier model (\ccwRate\% reversion), while conventions
whose reversal is itself taught (clockwise contour orientation, left-hand cross products)
are obeyed ($\le$\cwRate--\lhRate\%); a weighted-logistic trend across all eight
conventions gives $z=\tierZ$. (2) \emph{Cognitive load}: within a taught-reversal domain,
raising task difficulty reactivates the prior dose-dependently
(\cwEGptOss\%$\to$\cwHGemma\% as strain rises), while a transform-matched control
requiring the \emph{identical} output edit stays $\le$\wrkHGptOss--\wrkHGemma\%,
isolating convention entrenchment from generic instruction loss. (3) \emph{Capability}:
mid-scale models revert on even taught-reversal conventions ($91$--$99\%$) that frontier
models obey. Two mitigations fail: enabling reasoning raises competence by up to $76$
points while moving compliance by $2$--$4$ points (five model scales, two domains), and
in-context worked demonstrations of the required convention leave reversion essentially
unchanged in two of three frontier models (GPT-5: \shotZeroGptFive\%$\to$%
\shotThreeGptFive\% after three examples). Two pre-registered predictions were refuted
and are reported as such. All answers are graded sign-exactly against four-way
independent oracles; the benchmark regenerates deterministically by seed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A language model asked to analyze a circuit with mesh currents defined
counter-clockwise will often do everything right---set up the equations, solve the
system, recover every magnitude---and then report the signs a clockwise analysis would
have produced. The answer looks correct: units, structure, and magnitudes are all
right. Only a sign-exact check against the instructed convention reveals that the model
has silently substituted the convention it was trained on for the one it was told to
use. We call this failure mode \emph{convention reversion}, and this paper measures it,
maps what governs it, and tests what fails to fix it.

Sign conventions are an unusually clean probe of instruction following. They are
\emph{arbitrary} (both choices are physically valid, so the instruction is legitimate),
\emph{binary} (compliance is a sign), and \emph{exactly gradable} (a solver oracle can
compute the correct answer under either convention). Because magnitude and sign are
separately gradable, competence and compliance can be measured independently within a
single response---avoiding the confound in which instruction-following failures are
mistaken for capability failures, and vice versa.

\textbf{Contributions.}
(1)~\emph{An instrument}: three procedurally generated domains (Section~\ref{sec:inst})
in which every answer is verified by four independent solvers (three exact, zero
tolerance), every convention transform is independently re-realized (e.g., a left-handed
convention is validated by re-solving with the Levi-Civita symbol negated), and grading
is fully deterministic---no LLM judges anywhere.
(2)~\emph{A phenomenon}: reversion is silent and directed. \coherencePct\% of
\totalDiagVars{} sign errors match the trained frame exactly; the residual 0.15\%
occurs only under the study's single affine (non-sign-flip) transform
(Section~\ref{sec:directed}).
(3)~\emph{An anatomy}: reversion is governed by reversal pedagogy, load, and capability
(Section~\ref{sec:anatomy}), including a dose-response with a transform-matched control
that isolates entrenchment causally.
(4)~\emph{Two negative results on mitigation}: reasoning modes and in-context worked
demonstrations both fail to restore compliance (Section~\ref{sec:nofix}).
(5)~\emph{A falsification record}: two predictions pre-registered in the project
history were refuted by the data and are reported as refuted
(Section~\ref{sec:prereg}).

\section{The instrument}\label{sec:inst}
\textbf{Domains.} \emph{Circuits}: linear DC networks across seven topology families
(supermesh, dependent sources, bridges, ladders); requested variables are mesh currents,
node voltages, and branch quantities. \emph{Contour}: oriented line integrals of
polynomial vector fields around lattice polygons (including non-convex); requested
variables are per-edge work integrals, total circulation, and flux. \emph{Vectors}:
multi-body torque, angular-momentum, and magnetic-force systems in $\mathbb{R}^3$;
requested variables are vector components and invariant scalars. Difficulty is a config
knob (element count, polynomial degree, coordinate magnitude); each domain ships an
easy and a hardened tier.

\textbf{Conventions as contracts.} Each instance is posed under a \emph{convention
contract}: the default cell states the textbook convention explicitly; flip cells
change exactly one convention (e.g., ``mesh currents are defined COUNTER-CLOCKWISE'').
Eight conventions are instructed across the domains (Table~\ref{tab:entrench}). Every
variable carries a role determining which contracts flip it, giving per-cell
diagnostic masks; a solution-method contract (e.g., mesh vs.\ nodal analysis; direct
vs.\ transfer theorem) separates reporting-frame effects from procedure effects.
Critically, two contour cells---clockwise orientation (\texttt{cw}) and
work-done-against-the-field (\texttt{wrk})---demand the \emph{byte-identical} output
transform (negate all work values) while naming different conventions: any behavioral
difference between them isolates convention identity with output demands perfectly
matched.

\textbf{Verification.} Every accepted instance passes a four-way oracle: in circuits,
two independent hand-derived formulations, an exact symbolic MNA solver, and NGSPICE;
in contour, symbolic parametrized integration, Green's-theorem double integration over
an exact triangulation, an independent rational-arithmetic engine, and numerical
quadrature; in vectors, component formulas, Levi-Civita summation, symbolic cross
products, and floating-point evaluation, plus exact transfer-theorem identities. Three
of four routes are exact rational arithmetic compared at zero tolerance; datasets
report \texttt{inconsistent}~$=0$ by construction. Expected answers under every
contract are additionally validated by \emph{independent physical realization}: the
clockwise cell by re-grounded re-simulation, the left-handed cell by re-solving with
$\epsilon_{ijk}\to-\epsilon_{ijk}$, the reaction cell by negating force sources
(125/125 physics PASS in each domain). Grading is deterministic: a mandated answer
line is parsed by rules; a prediction is \emph{reverted} only if it matches the
trained-frame value within tolerance where the instructed frame demanded the opposite
sign.

\textbf{Panel.} Fifteen configurations: GPT-5 (low reasoning effort), gpt-oss-120b,
Gemma-4-31B, DeepSeek-V3, Llama-3.3-70B, Qwen2.5-72B, and the Qwen3 dense ladder
(1.7B--32B) with its native reasoning toggle, spanning $\sim$790$\times$ in
whole-problem competence. Decoding policy is uniform; provenance (serving stack,
quantization) is pinned and recorded per row.

\section{Reversion is silent and directed}\label{sec:directed}
Across all domains, tiers, cells, and models we grade \totalDiagVars{}
magnitude-correct diagnostic variables. \coherencePct\% of all sign errors equal the
trained convention's value exactly; \incoherentN{} incoherent cases (0.15\%) occur, and
every one falls in the circuits reference-node cell---the study's only \emph{affine}
transform, where partially applying the potential shift produces a value matching
neither frame. On all seven pure sign-flip conventions, the incoherence count is zero.
Sign errors in these models are not noise: they are the training distribution showing
through, variable by variable.

The paired within-physics design makes the effect causal. In circuits, the same
physics posed under the default vs.\ counter-clockwise contract yields aggregate
discordant counts $b{=}401$ (correct at default, reverted at flip) vs.\ $c{=}6$ across
the panel, with exact McNemar $q_{\mathrm{BH}}\le 0.006$ in every competent family.
Competence does not immunize: Gemma-4-31B solves \circMagGemma\% of circuit magnitudes
yet reverts \circCcwGemma\% of diagnostic signs. The same models comply at 85--97\%
with matched \emph{non-convention} unusual instructions, and test--retest resampling
($k{=}5$) bounds run-to-run variation at a few points---the rate is a stable property,
not sampling luck.

\section{What governs reversion}\label{sec:anatomy}

\subsection{Reversal pedagogy: the entrenchment gradient}\label{sec:tier}
The eight conventions differ sharply in whether their \emph{reversal} appears in
training-adjacent text. We code each on an ordinal pedagogy tier from a documented
audit of standard textbooks: tier~0, the reversal is taught as a routine operation
(reversing a contour's orientation negates the integral---a theorem in every calculus
text; reaction forces; inward normals); tier~1, the reversal appears only as an
error-contrast or partial treatment (the left hand gives the opposite direction;
active sign convention; non-ground references); tier~2, the reversal is essentially
absent (no circuits textbook we audited works a counter-clockwise mesh example).
Table~\ref{tab:entrench} and Figure~\ref{fig:entrench} give the mapping against
measured frontier reversion at low strain: tier-0 conventions are obeyed
(\cwRate--\rxnRate\%), the tier-2 convention defeats every model (\ccwRate\%), and
tier-1 conventions fall between or split by model. A weighted logistic of reversion on
tier across all eight conventions gives $z=\tierZ$ ($p<10^{-6}$).

\subsection{Load: the dose-response, with a matched-transform control}\label{sec:dose}
Within the taught-reversal contour domain, we raise difficulty (polynomial degree
3--4, 5--8 edges) and measure reversion as competence strain rises. Reversion on the
orientation convention climbs monotonically---gpt-oss \cwEGptOss\%$\to$\cwHGptOss\% at
\strainHGptOss\% strain, GPT-5 \cwEGptFive\%$\to$\cwHGptFive\% at \strainHGptFive\%,
Gemma \cwEGemma\%$\to$\cwHGemma\% at \strainHGemma\% (retest mean 34.4\%,
SD 6.4pp)---while the \texttt{wrk} cell, which demands the \emph{identical} negation
of the same variables, stays at \wrkHGptFive--\wrkHGemma\%
(Figure~\ref{fig:dose}; weighted-logistic $z=\strainZ$). Load does not cause generic
instruction loss; it selectively reactivates the trained default. Survivorship in the
hardened tier is conservative: rows lost to truncation are the most-strained
instances.

\subsection{Capability: compliance is frontier-emergent}\label{sec:cap}
Mid-scale models revert even on tier-0 conventions frontier models obey: the Qwen3
ladder reverts $91$--$99\%$ on contour orientation at every scale and both reasoning
modes (Table~\ref{tab:c1}), and DeepSeek-V3, Llama-3.3-70B, and Qwen2.5-72B revert
$92$--$94\%$---though all show large \texttt{cw}$>$\texttt{wrk} gaps (e.g., 92\% vs.\
35\%), so convention identity matters even below the competence floor. Following a
taught reversal under load is itself an emergent capability.

\section{What does not fix it}\label{sec:nofix}
\textbf{Reasoning.} Toggling Qwen3's native reasoning mode raises contour
magnitude-competence from 0--15\% to 24--88\% across five scales while moving
orientation reversion by $2$--$4$ points (Table~\ref{tab:c1},
Figure~\ref{fig:c1})---replicating, in a second domain, the dissociation we first
observed in circuits. Test-time compute purchases competence, not compliance.

\textbf{Demonstrations.} We prepend one or three fully worked, solver-verified
examples of the counter-clockwise convention (held-out physics, different topologies)
to the same circuit instances. Gemma declines shallowly
(\shotZeroGemma\%$\to$\shotOneGemma\%$\to$\shotThreeGemma\%, $\sim$4.5pp per
example); gpt-oss (\shotZeroGptOss\%$\to$\shotThreeGptOss\%) and GPT-5
(\shotZeroGptFive\%$\to$\shotOneGptFive\%$\to$\shotThreeGptFive\%) do not move
(Figure~\ref{fig:kshot}). The prior is decode-dominant: showing the model exactly the
behavior required, with verified answers, does not override it. The mitigation
practitioners would reach for first does not meaningfully work.

\section{Pre-registered predictions, both refuted}\label{sec:prereg}
Two predictions were committed to the project's version history before the
corresponding data existed. \textbf{(P1)} We predicted left-handed cross products
would behave like counter-clockwise mesh currents (an untrained reversal) and revert
heavily. Refuted: across three difficulty tiers and $\sim$3{,}700 diagnostic
variables, frontier reversion is \lhGptOss--\lhGptFive\% ($0/\lhNGemma$ for Gemma at
95\% competence). The left-hand rule is taught---as an error-contrast---in essentially
every introductory text; the refutation is what forced the ordinal pedagogy coding of
Section~\ref{sec:tier}. \textbf{(P2)} We predicted in-context demonstrations would
collapse reversion if it stems from missing training text. Refuted
(Section~\ref{sec:nofix}). We report both because a framework that only ever confirms
itself is not measuring anything; the surviving claims are the ones these refutations
disciplined.

\section{Related work}
Instruction-following benchmarks measure compliance with formatting and content
constraints \citep{zhou2023ifeval}; counter-default instructions have been studied as
``inverse'' instructions \citep{stephan2024inverse} and counterfactual task variants
\citep{wu2024reasoning}. Our contribution is orthogonal machinery: sign-exact oracles
that separate compliance from competence within one response, matched-transform
controls, and pedagogy-tiered conventions. Sycophantic deference to user framing
\citep{sharma2023sycophancy} is related but our effect persists with no user stance to
defer to. Reasoning-scaling work \citep{muennighoff2025s1,deepseekr1} measures
capability gains from test-time compute; we show those gains do not transfer to
convention compliance. The benchmark extends a published single-domain probe
\citep{ravishankara2026circuchain}, and follows deterministic-evaluation practice from
engineering-plot benchmarks \citep{ravishankara2026plotchain}; canary-based
contamination protection follows \citet{srivastava2022bigbench}. Statistical machinery
uses exact McNemar tests \citep{mcnemar1947} and Wilson intervals \citep{wilson1927}.

\section{Discussion}
\textbf{Why it matters.} These failures are silent (magnitudes correct), directed
(always toward the trained frame), load-sensitive (worst exactly when problems are
hard), and prompt-robust (demonstrations do not help). In engineering workflows a
sign is the difference between stable and unstable, tension and compression, charge
and discharge. Evaluations that grade magnitudes, final answers modulo sign, or use
LLM judges will systematically miss this class.

\textbf{Limitations.} The tier-2 pole is occupied by one convention; our search for a
second (P1) instead found a tier-1 case, and we report the gradient rather than a
binary law. Strain could not be induced in the vectors domain (95--100\% competence
at all tiers), so the taught-reversal$\times$high-strain cell is measured only in
contour. Pedagogy tiers are coded from a documented but necessarily finite textbook
audit. Serving stacks are mixed across the panel (pinned and recorded); hardened-tier
truncation censors the most-strained rows conservatively.

\textbf{Release.} The benchmark regenerates byte-identically from seeds; we release
all generators, oracles, validators, and analysis code publicly, and the frozen
instances and model outputs under gated access with embedded canaries---because a
benchmark that measures a training-frequency effect inverts, rather than degrades,
under contamination.

\section{Reproducibility}
All datasets are deterministic functions of (config, seed); regeneration commands,
manifests with canary GUIDs, transform-validation reports (125/125 per domain), the
complete statistical freeze (\texttt{paper\_stats.json}), and per-row provenance
